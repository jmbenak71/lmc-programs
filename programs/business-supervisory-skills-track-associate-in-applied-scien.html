About the Program

The AAS in Business program prepares graduates for entry-level, independent, and supervisory positions in business and can accelerate career opportunities for experienced professionals. Offered in both traditional and fully online formats, the program allows you to customize your education with a specialty track in Digital Marketing, Gig Economy, Small Business Management, Sales and Customer Service, Supervisory Skills, Data Analytics, Computer Information Systems, or Risk Management and Insurance.

Program Outcomes

Graduates will be able to:

  1. 1Apply professional behaviors -- including dependability, initiative, adaptability, and self-branding -- to perform effectively in workplace settings.
  2. 2Demonstrate effective communication across oral, written, digital, and nonverbal channels using business formats and conventions.
  3. 3Collaborate in diverse teams using tools and methodologies to achieve business goals.
  4. 4Evaluate ethical issues using best-practice methodologies to inform responsible business decisions.
  5. 5Apply core business concepts to design practical solutions for business problems.
  6. 6Leverage business technologies and data analysis to support data-driven decision making.

BUSA 103 · 3 credits

Introduction to Business

Provides an overview of fundamental business concepts and practices. Students will be introduced to key topics such as management, marketing, finance, entrepreneurship, operations, and business ethics. The course explores the role of businesses in society, the global economy, and the various forms of business ownership. Students will also learn about decision-making, strategic planning, and the implications of the dynamic business environment. This course helps students develop a business vocabulary essential for more advanced courses. The course also assists students in deciding if the business major is appropriate for their aspiration and, if so, what specific area to pursue.

Prerequisites: None

BUSA 203 · 3 credits

Principles of Economics (Macro)

Explore the broader economy by focusing on core economic principles and assumptions. Analyze how unemployment, inflation, and economic growth are measured and how they align with economic objectives. Delve into fiscal and monetary policies to understand how government actions influence the business cycle. Develop a comprehensive understanding of macroeconomic tools, applying them to various contexts while evaluating diverse perspectives on government involvement. Apply skills to analyze current social issues from an economic perspective.

Prerequisites: ENGL 101 (may be taken concurrently) and MATH 123 or higher (may be taken concurrently) or equivalent minimum test score: ACT Math 23, SAT Math 550 (composite), SAT Math 27.5 (section), Accuplacer Advanced Algebra 237
