About the Program

The Manufacturing Production Certificate provides basic and advanced manufacturing skills. This certificate allows you to enter the job market with basic, entry-level skills needed to be effective in the workforce.

Program Outcomes

Graduates will be able to:

  1. 1Demonstrate safe work habits and be conscious of safety when working with equipment and machinery related to the students focused area of study.
  2. 2Read blueprints, interpret drawings, understand specifications, and establish tolerances related to the students focused area of study.
  3. 3Apply mathematics in their focused area of study (Electrical, Maintenance, Advance Manufacturing, Welding, etc.)
  4. 4Operate basic tools and demonstrate knowledge of their use as they relate to the focused area of study.
  5. 5Demonstrate skills on computers, and digital technology used in their focused area of study. (Robotics, CNC, PLC, Meters, CAD, CAM, etc.)
  6. 6Operate machinery; select and plan machine operations on equipment used in their focused area of study.
  7. 7Demonstrate skills in quality control, inspection, gauging methods, and production control as they relate to their focused area of study.
  8. 8Demonstrate basic oral communications skills, speak logically, and use various types of oral and written communication techniques to promote good business relationships, to develop leadership, and to establish good employer-employee relationships.
  9. 9Describe conduct that is required for on-the-job training programs.
  10. 10Describe how their chosen career path will help shape other human behaviors and how their career effects local community and the world as a whole for the better.
  11. 11Recognize others creativity and the craftsmanship necessary to produce an industrial product.
  12. 12Recognize how their chosen career path will require updated training as technology and equipment continue to advance.

MACH 110 · 1–3 credits

Machine Tool I

Introductory course includes machining theory, demonstrations and shop experience. Basics in safety, blueprint reading, layout, band sawing, machine setup, lathe work, milling machine work, surface grinding.

Prerequisites: None · Additional Course Fee: $75.00

MANU 111 · 3 credits

Manufacturing Process I

Introductory course includes historical perspective of manufacturing, materials processing, product development, material selection and business principles and functions as related to manufacturing.

Prerequisites: None

MANU 120 · 3 credits

Fundamentals of Programmable Controllers

Introductory course to familiarize students with Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) and Human Machine Interfaces (HMIs). Units include input/output capabilities, logic instructions, and creating, programming, and editing PLC and HMI.

Prerequisites: None · Additional Course Fee: $50.00

Blueprint Reading & Sketching

Basics of interpreting working drawings, tolerancing, machining symbols, fasteners, sections, auxiliary views, developments, piping drawing, material specifications, casting drawings, assembly drawings, welding drawings and machine elements.

Introduction to Welding

A hands-on survey course of common welding and cutting processes, including Gas Metal Arc Welding, Shielded Metal Arc Welding, Gas Tungsten Arc Welding, Oxyfuel Cutting, Plasma Arc Cutting, and Carbon Arc Gouging.

Prerequisites: None · Additional Course Fee: $120.00
